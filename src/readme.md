

# 📚 BookNook — Your Personal Book Hub

**BookNook** is an online book management platform where readers can explore books, discover new arrivals, add books to the collection, and manage their borrowing activities.

🌐 **Live Website:** [https://booknook-client-nu.vercel.app/](https://booknook-client-nu.vercel.app/)

## Features

- 🔐 **Secure & Seamless Authentication** — Sign in easily with email or Google.
- 📚 **Explore Your Next Favorite Book** — Discover a growing collection of books in one place.
- 🏷️ **Smart Category Filtering** — Find books quickly by browsing your favorite categories.
- ✨ **Fresh Reads, Always** — Discover the latest additions and never miss a new arrival.
- 📖 **Effortless Book Management & Borrowing** — Add books, manage your collection, and keep track of your borrowed books.
